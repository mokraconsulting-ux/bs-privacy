Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Beyond Strategy entry pass app uses, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. The app is run by MOKRA Consulting, a student-led consulting club, for its event "Beyond Strategy: Consulting & Career Lab" on 12 December 2026.

What we collect

  • Your registration details from our Google Form: your name, email address, phone number, university, faculty and year of study.
  • Your Google account email address, if you choose "Continue with Google". Google tells us which account you signed in with; we use only its email address.
  • Your check-in record: the time our committee scanned your pass and which committee member scanned it.
  • Small notes on your own device: the app remembers the email you signed in with and your "Pack your bag" ticks in your browser, so you don't have to sign in again. These stay on your device and you can clear them with "Sign out" or by clearing your browser data.

How we use it

  • To check that you are a confirmed participant and show you your personal entry pass and QR code.
  • To check you in on the event day and keep an accurate attendance list.
  • To contact you about this event if something changes.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, the app reads only your email address and only to find your pass. It does not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data. You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account under "Third-party apps & services".

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Your details are kept in a Google Sheet owned by MOKRA Consulting's Google account and processed by Google Apps Script. Google provides these services.
  • The app's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record basic technical information when you open a page, as any website host does.
  • Only MOKRA Consulting committee members running the event can see the attendee list.
  • Other attendees cannot see your details. Your pass shows only your name, university, faculty, attendee type and check-in status.

How long we keep it

We keep attendee and check-in records for up to 6 months after the event, for attendance records and follow-up about this event, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us. You can also sign in by typing your email instead of using Google sign-in.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
